What is a Business Owners Policy?

A Business Owners Policy combines two major types of coverage into a single package:

Property Insurance

Covers physical assets like your building, equipment, inventory, and furnishings.

General Liability Insurance

Protects against claims of bodily injury, property damage, and personal/advertising injury.

With one policy, you safeguard your business against common risks—allowing you to focus on growth, not uncertainty.

What Does a Business Owner's Policy Cover?

Property Coverage

Buildings & Structures

Whether you own or lease, the structure housing your business is protected from fire, theft, vandalism, and more.

Business Personal Property

Covers equipment, furniture, inventory, and fixtures inside your premises.

Loss of Income

If your business is forced to close due to a covered loss, this helps replace lost income and covers operating expenses.

Liability Coverage

Bodily Injury & Property Damage

Protects you if a customer is injured on your property or if your operations cause damage to someone else’s property.

Products & Completed Operations

Covers claims related to products you sell or services you provide after the work is done.

Personal & Advertising Injury

Protects against claims of libel, slander, copyright infringement, or false advertising.

How Much Does a Business Owner's Policy Cost in Florida?

The cost of a Business Owners Policy depends on:

Business type & industry
Size & location of your business
Property value and assets
Claims history
Chosen coverages and limits

On average, small businesses in Florida can expect to pay between $500 and $2,500 annually for a standard BOP.
