What is General Liability Insurance?

General Liability Insurance (GLI) covers legal and financial responsibilities if your business is found liable for injuries, property damage, or personal/advertising harm. It's one of the most essential types of coverage for any business interacting with clients, customers, or the public.

What Does General Liability Insurance Cover?

Bodily Injury

Covers medical costs, legal fees, and settlements if someone is injured on your premises or due to your business activities.

Property Damage

Pays for damage you or your employees cause to another person’s property during the course of business.

Personal & Advertising Injury

Protection from claims of libel, slander, copyright infringement, or false advertising.

Legal Defense Costs

Covers attorney fees, court costs, and settlements—even if you are not found liable.

Products & Completed Operations

Provides coverage for harm caused by products you’ve sold or services you’ve completed.

Who Needs General Liability Insurance?

Any Florida business that deals with clients, customers, or the public should carry GLI. This includes:

Retail Stores & Restaurants

Contractors & Tradespeople

Real Estate & Property Managers

Consultants & Freelancers

Medical Offices

Event Organizers

Even home-based businesses can benefit from GLI, as homeowner’s insurance typically excludes business-related claims.

How Much Does General Liability Insurance Cost in Florida?

Premiums vary depending on:

Business size & Type
Location & Risk Exposure
Claims History
Coverage limits & Deductibles
Number of employees

On average, small businesses in Florida pay between $500 and $1,500 annually for basic GLI coverage.

What is Not Covered by General Liability Insurance?

While GLI offers broad protection, some risks require additional policies:

Professional Mistakes

Covered by Professional Liability (Errors & Omissions) Insurance.

Employee Injuries

Covered by Workers' Compensation.

Vehicle Accidents

Covered by Commercial Auto Insurance.

Property Damage to Your Business

Covered by Commercial Property Insurance.
