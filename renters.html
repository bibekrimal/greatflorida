Renters Insurance

Your landlord can insure the structure of your home or apartment. But inside the walls is up to you. Your prized possessions and childhood collections can all be covered with a renters insurance policy in Florida. You can even spare yourself legal and medical costs if someone gets hurt in your home.

How is Renters Insurance Different From Homeowners Insurance?

Homeowners insurance covers the structure of a property. But if you don't own your home, your belongings won't be covered from theft, fire, damage, or accidents. Renters insurance can even cover the cost of moving after a disaster.

What Does Renters Insurance Cover in Florida?

If you rent, your space still needs protection from the unexpected. Whether you live in a house, condo, or apartment, renters insurance can cover you for:

Liability

If a visitor is injured in your home, you may be liable for legal, medical, and other costs. Renters insurance protects you and your guests from financial and relationship crisis.

Personal Property

Thieves, fire and water damage don't care whether you rent or own. Protect your furniture, valuables, and appliances with a completely customized renters insurance policy.

Relocation Expenses

An unexpected move is stressful, but it can also be expensive. Renters insurance policies can cover relocation expenses in the event of a disaster.

If Someone is Hurt in My Home, Can Renters Insurance Help?

Yes. Legal costs and medical bills can be devastating. But renters insurance provides the protection you need if someone gets hurt in your home. It also ensures your guests can get the crucial care they might need if they are injured in your home.

What is a Deductible?
