Why You Need RV Insurance in Florida

Living the RV life in Florida is a blast—think endless sunshine, epic campgrounds, and coastal road trips. But with great adventures come a few risks. Florida law says if your RV has a motor (like a Class A, B, or C motorhome), you need at least basic coverage to hit the road legally. Even if you’re towing a travel trailer or fifth wheel, insurance isn’t just smart—it’s peace of mind. What Does GreatFlorida RV Insurance Cover?

We partner with top-notch insurers to build policies that fit your RV lifestyle—whether you’re a weekend warrior or a full-time roadie. Here’s the rundown on what we can cover:

Liability Coverage

Required for motorized RVs in Florida, this pays for damage or injuries you cause to others—like if you accidentally ding someone’s car at a campsite. Minimums are $10,000 for property damage and $10,000 for Personal Injury Protection (PIP).

Collision Coverage

Fixes your RV if you bump into a tree, guardrail, or another vehicle. Perfect for those “oops” moments on tight backroads.

Comprehensive Coverage

Covers the wild stuff—like theft, vandalism, or a rogue branch crashing through your roof during a storm. Florida’s weather can be a doozy, so this one’s a fan favorite.

Personal Injury Protection (PIP)

Florida’s no-fault law means you need this—it covers medical bills for you and your passengers, no matter who’s at fault. $10,000 is the minimum, but we can tweak it higher if you want extra cushion.

Uninsured Motorist Coverage

With so many drivers on Florida roads, this steps in if someone without insurance (or not enough) hits you. Trust us, it’s a lifesaver.

Personal Belongings

Your gear—think TVs, camping chairs, or that fancy coffee maker—gets protection if it’s damaged or stolen.

Roadside Assistance

Flat tire in the Everglades? Dead battery near Orlando? We’ll get you towed or fixed, 24/7.

Full-Time RV Living

If your RV is your permanent pad, we’ll add extras like liability for when you’re parked—similar to homeowners coverage.

Ask us about add-ons like emergency expense coverage (for hotels if your RV’s out of commission) or pet injury protection for your furry co-pilot.

Florida RV Insurance: What’s Required?

Florida keeps it straightforward for motorized RVs:

$10,000 Property Damage Liability (PDL)

Covers stuff you might smash into.

$10,000 Personal Injury Protection (PIP)

Takes care of medical costs, no questions asked.

Towing a trailer? You’re not required to insure it separately, but if it’s registered, you’ll need to show proof of coverage (like liability and PIP) to stay legal. Either way, our agents recommend going beyond the minimums—Florida’s roads and weather can throw curveballs, and we’d rather see you fully protected than stuck with a big bill.
